Your wedding deserves more than a paper card.

Paper invitations get lost, details change, and guests call you the night before asking for directions. An Ever After website gathers everything into one gorgeous link you share on WhatsApp, designed in your colours, your style and your story.

Everything your guests need, in one link.

  • Tap-to-open invitation

    A wax-sealed digital card that opens with a touch, styled to your theme.

  • Live countdown

    Days, hours and minutes to "I do", ticking for every guest.

  • RSVP

    Guests reply in seconds, so you know exactly who's coming.

  • Venues and directions

    Ceremony and reception details with one-tap map directions.

  • Dress code and colours

    Show your colours so every guest arrives looking the part.

  • Gift details

    Mobile money and bank details guests can copy with one tap.

  • Save the date

    One tap adds your wedding straight to their calendar.

  • Private to your guests

    Shared only with the people you invite, never listed publicly.
